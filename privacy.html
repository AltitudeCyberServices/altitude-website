Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Altitude ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you contact or use our services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with Altitude, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and business name
  • Your business email address
  • Contact information you voluntarily provide
  • Information provided during a cybersecurity readiness assessment
  • Information contained in communications between you and Altitude

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide and manage our services
  • Prepare cybersecurity readiness reports
  • Communicate with clients regarding their assessment
  • Improve our services and website
  • Maintain appropriate business records

3. Cybersecurity Assessment Information

Information provided as part of an assessment will only be used for the purpose of carrying out the agreed service and preparing the resulting report.

Altitude does not require passwords, authentication credentials, payment-card information or other unnecessary sensitive information to conduct a standard readiness assessment.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may only be shared with third parties where reasonably necessary to provide a service, comply with a legal obligation, protect our rights, or where you have otherwise given permission.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of transmitting or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Personal information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including fulfilling business, legal and accounting requirements.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable UK data protection law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of your information.

9. Changes to This Policy
