1. About Altitude

Altitude provides student-led cybersecurity readiness and cybersecurity awareness services for businesses.

Our services are intended to help businesses identify common areas for improvement and understand practical steps they can take to strengthen their cybersecurity posture.

2. Our Services

Services are provided based on the specific assessment or service agreed with the client.

A standard cybersecurity readiness assessment may consider areas such as:

  • Account and authentication security
  • User access and permissions
  • Backup practices
  • Device security
  • Basic security procedures
  • Cybersecurity awareness

3. Scope of Assessments

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing, Altitude's standard readiness assessment does not include penetration testing, exploitation of vulnerabilities, vulnerability scanning of systems, malware analysis, digital forensics, incident response or unauthorised access to systems.

Clients must not provide passwords, authentication credentials, payment-card information or other unnecessary sensitive information as part of a standard assessment.
